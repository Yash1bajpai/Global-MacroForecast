HONEST EVALUATION

Evaluation & Limitations

What these forecasts are, how future models will be judged, and what the numbers can and cannot tell you.

Why no accuracy scores are shown

The deployed models are legacy. Their old scores mixed different forecast horizons, used inputs that would not have been available at prediction time, and had blend weights chosen on the test period, so they flattered the models. They were removed rather than relabeled.

  • Current status: the same legacy models still serve the forecasts above, unchanged and labeled experimental.
  • Corrected evaluation: a leakage-safe rolling-origin pipeline now scores new candidates. None has beaten the simple baselines (training mean, last value, seasonal naive), so nothing new has been promoted.
  • Open question: the deployed models have not been re-scored under the corrected protocol, so their true skill is unknown in either direction.

How future models are evaluated

Candidate models are evaluated so they cannot cheat, and none is promoted unless it beats every baseline:

  • Temporal Train/Test Split: strict chronological splitting. Test data is always from a future period never seen during training.
  • No Future Feature Leakage: lags and rolling stats are strictly past-dated.
  • Fail-Closed Promotion Gate: a candidate must beat the training-mean, last-value and seasonal baselines on validation and test before it can replace the legacy models.
